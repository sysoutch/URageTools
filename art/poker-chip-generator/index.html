Pixel Poker Chips

Procedural pixel-art chips with k-means palette quantization

Tip: Change the seed to reproduce a particular set. Every chip is generated procedurally, so there are thousands of possible combinations of colors, patterns, markings and styles. K-Means quantization snaps each render to a small flat-color palette for crisp pixel-art edges — try 4 colors for a chunky retro look. Every chip is shown in three views: exact top (flat, no lighting), diagonal front and dead-front edge-on — download any view as PNG.
